What we do

We don’t just deliver the instrument.

At Bernatali Inventions we guarantee precision, efficiency and hassle-free lab operations. Our services boost performance, ensure compliance and sharpen the skills of the people using the equipment.

01

Equipment supply

Advanced, reliable instruments and technologies, specified against your application, throughput and budget rather than a catalogue page.

02

Maintenance & after-sales

After-sales service that keeps instruments performing long after installation, protecting the value of what you invested in.

03

Calibration & compliance

Calibration that holds equipment inside specification and keeps your laboratory ready for audit and accreditation.

04

Practical training

Hands-on training that sharpens operator skill, reduces avoidable error and gets more out of the instruments already on your bench.

05

Chemicals & consumables

High-purity chemicals and the consumables a laboratory reorders every month, supplied with consistency and care.

06

Laboratory design

Modern, efficient and safe laboratory spaces, planned around how your team actually moves through the work.
